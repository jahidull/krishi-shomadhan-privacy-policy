Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: May 2026

📋 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Krishi Shomadhan ("we," "our," or "the app") protects your privacy when you use our mobile application.

Krishi Shomadhan is designed to work 100% offline. This means your data stays on your device and is not uploaded to any external server.

🔒 Privacy First

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Images and notes used inside the app remain on your device only.

📊 Data Collection and Usage

1. No Personal Data

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or location.
  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • We do not send your personal data to any server.

2. Camera Access

  • Purpose: The app may request camera permission only to capture images of crop leaves for AI-based disease detection.
  • Supported crops: Rice, Potato, and Corn.
  • Processing: Captured images are processed locally on your device.
  • No upload: These images are not uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

3. Local Storage

  • The app may use local storage to save agricultural notes, crop information, and calendar reminders.
  • This information stays on your device and never leaves your device.
  • You can delete this data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

🎯 How We Use Your Data

Krishi Shomadhan uses data only inside the app for the following purposes:

  1. Crop Disease Detection: To analyze crop leaf images locally and provide possible disease detection results.
  2. Agricultural Notes: To allow you to save your own farming notes on your device.
  3. Calendar Reminders: To help you manage agricultural activities and reminders locally.
  4. Offline App Functionality: To provide app features without internet connectivity.

💾 Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage Only: All app data is stored locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your data on cloud servers.
  • No External Transmission: The app does not transmit your images, notes, or reminders to us.
  • User Control: You can remove all locally stored data through Android settings or by uninstalling the app.
⚠️ No Third-Party Tracking

Krishi Shomadhan does not use third-party tracking or analytics tools that collect user data.

  • ❌ No advertising trackers
  • ❌ No analytics services
  • ❌ No user profiling
  • ❌ No selling or sharing of user data

🔑 Permissions Required

1. Camera Permission

  • Purpose: To capture crop leaf images for disease detection.
  • Optional Control: You may deny camera permission, but image-based disease detection may not work.

2. Storage / Local Data Access

  • Purpose: To save agricultural notes and calendar reminders locally.
  • Storage: Data is saved only on your device.

3. Notification Permission, if used

  • Purpose: To show calendar or farming activity reminders.
  • Control: You can disable notifications from your device settings.

👶 Children's Privacy

Krishi Shomadhan does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app works offline and does not transmit personal data, children’s privacy is protected.

⏱️ Data Retention

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • You can clear app data anytime from Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the app will remove locally stored app data from your device.

✅ Your Rights and Choices

  1. Access: You can view your notes and reminders inside the app.
  2. Modify: You can edit or update your locally saved information.
  3. Delete: You can delete data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  4. Permission Control: You can allow or deny app permissions from Android settings.

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page, and the “Last Updated” date will be revised accordingly.
